# Abide — a daily discipleship companion

A compact, phone-friendly companion for a service mission: scripture, Christlike attributes, physical and mental well-being, companionship, and gentle progress reflection.

Open `index.html` on the published site. On iPhone, open the HTTPS address in Safari, choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

Progress, reflections, and personal settings are stored in local browser storage on the device. They are not uploaded or synced. Avoid entering identifying details about people you serve. This repository and the hosted site are public.
